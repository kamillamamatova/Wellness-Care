Getting Started Is Easy

During our initial assessment, a registered nurse will answer all of your questions, fully explain our capabilities, assess you and/or your loved one’s needs, and develop the appropriate Plan of Care. We will jointly determine the scope of coverage required to be effective, whether it’s a 30 minute visit, or around-the-clock care or anywhere in between. We will diligently assure the right match with one of our dedicated staff and oversee the on-going care for quality assurance.

Discover Piece of Mind

We know that you have many choices when it comes to in-home care. We are confident that the more you get to know Priority Wellness Care the more you will realize how truly different we are from other in-home care providers.

Why Choose Us

We hand pick only those experienced individuals who display the spirit required and the attitude necessary to effectively help our clients. Hiring an independent caregiver may be a less costly alternative, but leaves the client with many unwanted responsibilities. It is vitally important for our clients to not only receive top-notch care, but to receive that care from a new-found friend.
